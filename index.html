01 currency_exchange ArbBot, a Polymarket and Kalshi arbitrage tracker PREDICTION MARKETS · REAL-TIME DATA · LIVE expand_more
What I built

A tracker that watches Polymarket and Kalshi for the same event priced two different ways.

How it works

Every 30 minutes it matches thousands of live markets across both sites. Every 5 seconds it rereads both order books, adds Kalshi's taker fee and a slippage buffer, and works out the true cost of holding YES on one side and NO on the other. When the pair costs under a dollar the payout is already larger than the stake, so it surfaces both legs with the numbers and can ping Discord.

My part

I built it end to end: the market matching, the pricing math, the dashboard, and the alerts. It reads public prices only and never places a trade.

What I built

A study of how well safety benchmarks actually catch unsafe model behaviour.

How it works

I ran more than 5,000 adversarial prompts through 50 separate safety benchmarks, scored where each benchmark caught a genuine problem and where it let one through, then grouped the misses into repeatable failure categories.

I was the undergraduate research assistant running the evaluations, and I wrote up the recurring blind spots so the benchmark design could be tightened.
